About CampusTrack

Problem

University students may struggle to track activities, class topics and important deadlines when their notes are spread across multiple places.

Solution

CampusTrack presents a simple linked website with a study task form, a sample course table and demonstration login and signup forms.

Project Objectives

  • Create an easy-to-use static website.
  • Apply HTML forms, tables, lists and navigation.
  • Use inline, internal and external CSS.
  • Apply JavaScript functions, DOM and events.
