Welcome to CampusTrack

CampusTrack is a static educational website that helps students keep their study plans and course information in one place.

Create a Study Task See Features

What Can You Do?

1. Study Planner

Add a task with a course name and due date.

2. Course Information

See a sample table of university study subjects.

3. Student Forms

Try the demonstration Login and Sign Up forms.

Why We Created This Website

Students may forget tasks and deadlines when they are written in different places. Our proposed solution is a simple website with linked pages and a task list.

Steps for a Better Routine

  1. Choose the course.
  2. Write your next task.
  3. Review your study list regularly.
